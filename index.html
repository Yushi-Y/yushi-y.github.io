Yushi Yang

Yushi is a PhD student in Social Data Science at the University of Oxford, where she is a member of the Reasoning with Machines Lab under the supervision of Prof. Adam Mahdi. Her research investigates post-training mechanisms for aligning language model agents, in particular how reinforcement learning and preference tuning methods shape model behaviours. Her work spans LLM post-training, agentic reinforcement learning, and AI alignment, and has been published at venues including EMNLP and ICML.

Alongside her doctoral research, Yushi joined Meta Superintelligence Labs as a Research Scientist Intern. She has previously worked as an AI Engineer at Reply, a Data Analyst at UNDP, and a Machine Learning Researcher at the University of Oxford and Imperial College London.

Research Interests: Large language models; Post-training; Agents; AI alignment; Reinforcement learning
