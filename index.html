About Me

Yushi is a DPhil student in Social Data Science at the OII, focusing on LLM post-training, LLM agents, and technical AI safety at the Reasoning with Machines Lab (led by Prof. Adam Mahdi). She holds an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from Imperial College London and an MSc in Statistical Science from Oxford.

Yushi has worked as an AI engineer at Reply, a data analyst with UNDP and an AI researcher at the University of Oxford and Imperial College London.

Yushi has worked on many ML projects: building a privacy-preserving recommendation web app; tracking brand sentiment in online news on AWS; interpreting emotions to artworks with visual-language models.

Her skillsets include deep learning models, Python, PyTorch, SQL and AWS services.
