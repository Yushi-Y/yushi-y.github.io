Yushi Yang

Yushi is a PhD student in Social Data Science at the University of Oxford, where she is a member of the Reasoning with Machines Lab led by Prof. Adam Mahdi. Her research spans LLM post-training, agentic reinforcement learning, and AI alignment, published at EMNLP and ICML.

Alongside her doctoral research, Yushi joined Meta Superintelligence Labs as a Research Scientist Intern on the RL & Agents Team. She has previously worked as an AI Engineer at Reply, a Data Analyst at UNDP, and a Machine Learning Researcher at the University of Oxford and Imperial College London.
